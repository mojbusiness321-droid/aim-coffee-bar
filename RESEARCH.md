## 6. Customer Sentiment & Vibe Analysis

- **Atmosphere & Seating:**
  - Modernist interior with polished concrete, stainless steel, and warm timber accents.
  - Generous power outlets and ergonomic seating popular with remote workers, tech employees, and university students in Al Malqa during morning and afternoon.
  - Outdoor patio that becomes vibrant in the evenings after 7:00 PM.
- **Top Reviewed Items:**
  - The **V60** is consistently praised for clarity and clean acidity.
  - The **Iced Spanish Latte** has a loyal following for balanced sweetness without overpowering the espresso.
  - The **Madrid Cheesecake** (San Sebastian style) is frequently ordered with black filter coffee.
  - The **Burrata Sandwich** is an afternoon crowd favorite.
